# SERP Analysis & Search Landscape — StudyAI Hub

## 1. SERP Analysis Methodology
To build an organic search strategy that earns first-page visibility, we analyze the current Search Engine Results Page (SERP) layout for our primary focus keywords. 

### Key Inspection Parameters
1. **Page Types Dominating Rankings:** (e.g., Aggregator directories, listicles, university resource pages, individual SaaS product homepages).
2. **Title Patterns:** Length, presence of years (e.g., "2026"), use of power words ("Free", "Best", "Top").
3. **SERP Features:** Featured Snippets, People Also Ask (PAA) boxes, Knowledge Panels, Video carousels.
4. **Competitor Strengths:** High domain authority (DR 70+), deep backlink profiles, comprehensive listicle counts (30-50 tools).
5. **Competitor Content Gaps:** Superficial descriptions, outdated pricing, neglect of academic integrity policies, lack of interactive filtering.

## 3. Recurring "People Also Ask" (PAA) Patterns
Across target queries, search engines consistently trigger the following PAA questions:
1. *What is the best AI tool for college students?*
2. *Can students use AI for homework without plagiarizing?*
3. *What are the best free AI tools for writing essays?*
4. *Which AI tool is best for research papers and literature review?*
5. *Is ChatGPT free for students?*
6. *Can AI generate PowerPoint presentations with citations?*

**Strategic Action:** Every PAA question identified is directly integrated into our category page and blog FAQ accordions with Schema.org `FAQPage` structured data.

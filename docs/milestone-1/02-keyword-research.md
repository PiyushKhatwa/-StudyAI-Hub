# 02 — Keyword Research & Strategy

## 1. Keyword Research Methodology & Data Integrity Standard
In strict compliance with CSET489 academic guidelines, search volume and Keyword Difficulty (KD) metrics are not fabricated. Metrics requiring third-party professional SEO suites (such as Ahrefs, SEMrush, or Google Keyword Planner with active campaign billing) are categorized as `Candidate` and tagged `TO VERIFY`.

### Keyword Selection Criteria
1. **Academic Intent Alignment:** Queries must reflect genuine collegiate student tasks (studying, coding, research, writing, presenting, productivity).
2. **Search Intent Feasibility:** Prioritizing informational and commercial investigation queries that can be comprehensively satisfied by curated hub pages.
3. **Difficulty Target:** Identifying long-tail queries and niche qualifiers (e.g., "for college students", "for research papers") where topical authority can win against broad domain authority giants.
